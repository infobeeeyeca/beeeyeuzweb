Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?+
Standard 5–10 page business sites: 10–14 days. E-commerce stores: 3–5 weeks. Complex custom applications: we scope it properly in your free consultation. We never give a timeline we can't commit to.
Do you work with businesses outside Tashkent?+
Yes — we work with businesses across all of Uzbekistan (Samarkand, Bukhara, Namangan, Andijan, Fergana) and internationally. Everything runs remotely via Telegram, email, and video calls. Location is irrelevant to output quality.
Will I be able to update my own website?+
Yes. Every website includes a CMS so you can update content, images, prices, and pages without touching code. We include a hands-on training session after launch so your team is fully independent from day one.
How do payments work? Do you accept Uzbek methods?+
We accept Payme, Click, Uzbek bank transfers, international credit cards, and PayPal. Standard terms: 50% deposit to begin, balance on delivery. Fixed pricing — you'll never receive an invoice that surprises you.
What's included in the free website audit?+
A 30-minute structured session covering: page speed analysis, SEO health score, mobile usability, conversion blockers, competitor comparison, and a prioritised action plan. Delivered in Uzbek, Russian, or English. No sales pressure.
Do you offer ongoing support after launch?+
All projects include 30 days (or 60 days for e-commerce) of free support. After that, monthly maintenance plans start at $49/month covering hosting, security updates, backups, and minor content changes.
